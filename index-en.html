  • August days are warm but mountain nights are cool — come prepared.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience with Louise Hay's method?
Not at all. The program is designed for both beginners and those already familiar with the philosophy. We will guide you step by step through every practice and exercise.
Is it necessary to have a car to get around?
It is highly recommended to rent a car, as Mount Shasta is a rural area and the event takes place near Lake Siskiyou. However, during our June Zoom meeting, we will help coordinate carpooling among attendees to make logistics easier for everyone.
What should I wear or bring?
August days are warm but mountain nights are cool. We recommend comfortable clothing you can sit in on the floor, hiking shoes, and layers for the evening outdoor activities under the stars. Don't forget a reusable water bottle.
Can I pay cash for the optional sound therapy?
Yes — that specific session ($72 USD) must be paid in cash directly to the facilitator on the day of the event. Cards are not accepted for this optional add-on.
Can I connect with other attendees to share a car or lodging?
Absolutely. Once you reach out to Isabela, we can connect you with other participants so you can form groups for carpooling or shared accommodations.
